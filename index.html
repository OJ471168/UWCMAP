How to use the Map

🌍 Exploring Events

Switch between Map View to see locations and List View to see a monthly calendar agenda.

❤️ Using Favorites

Click the Heart Icon on any event card to save it. You can then click the "Saved" button at the top to see your personal shortlist. Your favorites are saved on this device.

🔎 Filtering

Use the sidebar to filter by Category, Facilitator, or Date. In List View, use the top bar to filter by Location (Continent & Country).
